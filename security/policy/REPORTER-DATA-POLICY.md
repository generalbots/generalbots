# Reporter data policy (LGPD)

- Lawful basis: legitimate interest in securing the software; reports are processed only for triage, remediation and credit.
- Data minimisation: name/handle, contact address, affected component and reproduction. No CPF, phone, employer or IP address is requested or stored.
- Retention: 24 months from acknowledgement, then deletion or irreversible anonymisation. The 90-day embargo does not extend retention.
- Access: the security owner and the assigned triager only; access is logged. Reports are never copied into public issues, advisories or feeds.
- Rights: access, correction, deletion and objection are honoured through the same private contact channel within 15 days.
- Hall of fame: a reporter is named only after explicit consent, which can be withdrawn at any time. Credit may be anonymous.
- International transfer: the reporting provider's processing region must be disclosed in its own privacy notice; no reporter data is replicated to the bulletin mirrors.
