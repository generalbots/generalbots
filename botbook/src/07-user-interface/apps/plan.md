# Plan 🟡 PREVIEW

> **Preview application.** Usable today, not yet part of the supported surface.

Plan is a board for tracking work in flight: what is being done, by whom, at what priority and how far along.

## What it does

| Capability | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Planning board** | A plan containing its work items |
| **Status** | Items move through stages — in progress, in review, completed |
| **Priority** | Items are ranked, including an urgent level |
| **Responsibility** | Each item has an owner |
| **Progress** | Items carry start information and a completion percentage |
| **Comments** | Discussion attached to the item rather than to a separate thread |

> **Language note.** Some of the shipped labels are in Portuguese (*Meu Plano*, *Em Andamento*, *Em Revisão*, *Concluído*, *Urgente*, *Responsável*). The app is usable without Portuguese, but the labels will look inconsistent until localisation lands.

## Choosing between the planning apps

| App | Shape |
|---|---|
| **Plan** | A board of work items with status, priority and progress |
| [Goals](./goals.md) | Objectives and key results |
| [Project](./project.md) | Projects with phases and deliverables |
| [Tasks](./tasks.md) | Individual tasks, including AI execution |

## Opening it

Plan is a **preview** application. Turn on the **Preview** switch in the left sidebar, then open **Plan** from the app menu.
