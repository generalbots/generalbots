# newsletter

## Workflow

1. Subject ≤50 chars and preview text that adds information, not repetition.
2. Opening line states why this matters now.
3. Sections with subheads, 2-4 short paragraphs each.
4. One primary CTA, repeated once; secondary links in a footer.
5. Plain-text version works without images.
6. Include an unsubscribe line and real sender identity.

## Quality checks

- The email is understandable with images off.
- One CTA dominates; no competing buttons.

## Output

Write the artefact to `output/` and report its path plus one line on what it contains. Never claim a result you did not open and check.
