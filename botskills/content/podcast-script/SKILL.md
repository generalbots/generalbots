# podcast-script

## Workflow

1. Cold open with the strongest 20 seconds of the episode.
2. Intro ≤45 s: who, what the listener gets, guest credibility.
3. Segments with clear transitions and 3-5 questions per guest segment.
4. Prepare follow-ups for likely vague answers.
5. Outro: recap one takeaway, where to find more, single CTA.
6. Mark timestamps and ad slots.

## Quality checks

- Every question is open and specific.
- The episode can be cut at segment boundaries.

## Output

Write the artefact to `output/` and report its path plus one line on what it contains. Never claim a result you did not open and check.
