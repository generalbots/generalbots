# brand-tone

## Workflow

1. Write 3-5 voice principles with one sentence each (e.g. direct, warm, never salesy).
2. Give do/don't pairs for vocabulary, sentence length and humour.
3. Channel notes: chat, email, social, docs.
4. Provide a short example per channel rewritten in the voice.
5. Keep accessibility: plain language, no idioms that do not translate.
6. Review output against the principles before delivery.

## Quality checks

- Every deliverable cites which principle drove its tone.
- No channel uses a voice the guide forbids.

## Output

Write the artefact to `output/` and report its path plus one line on what it contains. Never claim a result you did not open and check.
