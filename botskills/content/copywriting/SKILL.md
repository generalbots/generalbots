# copywriting

## Workflow

1. State the audience and the single action the copy must drive.
2. Lead with the outcome for the reader, then the mechanism, then the proof.
3. Short sentences, concrete verbs, no jargon; one idea per paragraph.
4. Offer variants: a direct headline and a curiosity headline.
5. CTA is specific (Start free trial) not generic (Submit).
6. Remove every claim that cannot be supported.

## Quality checks

- The CTA appears once and is unambiguous.
- No superlatives without evidence.

## Output

Write the artefact to `output/` and report its path plus one line on what it contains. Never claim a result you did not open and check.
