# memes

## Workflow

1. Match the template's implied setup to the joke; do not force it.
2. Caption: short, concrete, no explanation of the joke.
3. Avoid punching down; check the audience and brand context.
4. Use a licensed template or the user's own image.
5. Keep text in the template's safe areas.
6. Deliver the image plus alt text describing the visual.

## Quality checks

- The joke is understandable without extra context.
- Template rights are respected.

## Output

Write the artefact to `output/` and report its path plus one line on what it contains. Never claim a result you did not open and check.
