# thumbnails

## Workflow

1. Use 1280x720 (16:9); one focal subject and one short title.
2. Title ≤4 words, large enough to read at 320 px wide.
3. High contrast between text and background; add a subtle scrim if needed.
4. Keep faces/eyes in the safe area; avoid edge-bleed text.
5. Export JPG <200 KB (YouTube limit is 2 MB).
6. Check the thumbnail at 320 px before delivery.

## Quality checks

- Title legible at 320 px.
- No text clipped at the platform's crop.

## Output

Write the artefact to `output/` and report its path plus one line on what it contains. Never claim a result you did not open and check.
