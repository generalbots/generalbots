# presentation-design

## Workflow

1. Set a 12-column grid with 64 px side margins and a 48 px title band.
2. Define three layout types: statement, content, data; reuse them.
3. One message per slide; title states the conclusion, not the topic.
4. Charts: one series highlighted, the rest muted; annotate the takeaway.
5. Speaker notes carry the narrative; slides stay sparse.
6. Verify by viewing a slide at 20% zoom: the message must still land.

## Quality checks

- No slide has more than one focal point.
- Margins and title baseline are identical across slides.

## Output

Write the artefact to `output/` and report its path plus one line on what it contains. Never claim a result you did not open and check.
