# logo-identity

## Workflow

1. Start from 3 concepts on a 1:1 grid; test each at 16 px before refining.
2. Build a primary lockup plus a stacked and an icon-only variant.
3. Provide monochrome and inverted versions; keep clear space = cap height.
4. Define the palette, minimum sizes and do/don't usage.
5. Export SVG (vector) + PNG at 512 and 64 px.
6. Verify legibility at favicon size.

## Quality checks

- The mark is recognisable in one colour at 16 px.
- No gradients or effects that vanish in print.

## Output

Write the artefact to `output/` and report its path plus one line on what it contains. Never claim a result you did not open and check.
