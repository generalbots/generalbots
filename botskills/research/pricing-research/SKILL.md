# pricing-research

## Workflow

1. Identify the exact plan/tier and billing period (monthly vs annual).
2. Quote the vendor page directly and capture the retrieval date.
3. Note currency, taxes and add-ons that change the effective price.
4. Compare on the same basis (per seat, per month, annualised).
5. Flag prices that require contacting sales as 'not public'.
6. Give a range when the vendor changes prices by region.

## Quality checks

- Prices are comparable units.
- Every number has a source and date.

## Output

Write the artefact to `output/` and report its path plus one line on what it contains. Never claim a result you did not open and check.
