# research-synthesis

## Workflow

1. State the decision the research must support.
2. Group findings by claim, not by source; note where sources agree.
3. Record conflicts with each side's evidence and date.
4. Weight by source quality (primary > vendor > blog) and recency.
5. Name the gaps that could change the decision.
6. End with a recommendation and the confidence level.

## Quality checks

- Every claim in the summary traces to a cited source.
- Conflicts are shown, not averaged away.

## Output

Write the artefact to `output/` and report its path plus one line on what it contains. Never claim a result you did not open and check.
