# contracts-review

## Workflow

1. List the parties, object, term, value and termination conditions.
2. Check the risky clauses: liability caps, indemnity, IP ownership, confidentiality, auto-renewal, penalties.
3. Compare obligations: what each side must do and by when.
4. Flag missing clauses (data protection, dispute resolution, change control).
5. Summarise in plain language with clause references.
6. State clearly that this is a review, not legal advice.

## Quality checks

- Every risk cites the clause number.
- Missing protections are listed, not assumed present.

## Output

Write the artefact to `output/` and report its path plus one line on what it contains. Never claim a result you did not open and check.
