# hr-onboarding

## Workflow

1. Pre-start: contract, documents, payroll data, equipment.
2. Day one: accounts, email, repositories, chat, calendar.
3. Least-privilege access by role; log every grant.
4. First week: pairing sessions, codebase tour, first small task.
5. 30/60/90 expectations written down.
6. Collect feedback at the end of week one.

## Quality checks

- Access matches the role, no shared credentials.
- The new hire has a named buddy and a first task.

## Output

Write the artefact to `output/` and report its path plus one line on what it contains. Never claim a result you did not open and check.
