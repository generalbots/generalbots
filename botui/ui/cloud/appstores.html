Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to provide?
Your app binary (.aab for Android, .ipa for iOS), app icon, screenshots, description, and any necessary API credentials. We guide you through each step.
How long does the process take?
Google Play review typically takes 1–3 days. Apple App Store review takes 1–7 days. Account creation takes 24–48 hours for Apple, instant for Google.
What if my app gets rejected?
We handle resubmissions at no additional cost. Our team ensures your app meets all store guidelines before the first submission.
Do I keep ownership of the developer account?
Yes! The developer account is created in your name with your credentials. We only assist in the setup and submission process.
Can I publish without a developer account yet?
Absolutely. We include the account setup consultation in the package. For Google Play ($25 one-time) and Apple ($99/year), the store fees are separate.
